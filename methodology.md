# Methodology Document: Pune Industrial Lead Generation

## 1. Sourcing Process & Data Ingestion
To isolate companies within the ₹50Cr to ₹500Cr revenue target without sorting through public noise, we focused strictly on Pune's major industrial manufacturing clusters: Chakan, Bhosari, Hadapsar, and Talegaon.
* Initial target candidates were cross-referenced using corporate tracking databases (Tofler and ZaubaCorp) to filter out enterprise-tier companies and micro-units.
* Company manufacturing catalogs were reviewed directly to verify physical asset placement.

## 2. Gate Screening & Evaluation
* Gate E1 (Producer Verification): Inspected product pages to check for physical plant equipment. Pure trading hubs and software agencies were flagged as immediate auto-disqualifications.
* Gate E2 (Accessibility): Confirmed direct physical infrastructure and operations within Indian borders.

## 3. Core Insights
Mid-market engineering firms in the Pune auto cluster maintain robust Systems Maturity (C7) because of strict Tier-1 automotive compliance mandates from major OEMs like Tata Motors and Bajaj. However, leadership structures remain highly centralized within the core family promoters, representing prime opportunities for DeepThought's organizational execution frameworks.
